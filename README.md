# Last.FM-Extended-spotify-history-importer
VIBE CODED WITH CLAUDE SONET 5.5 - A small Python/Tkinter app that takes your Spotify extended streaming history, drops short listens, cuts it into sublists of 2,000 tracks, and scrobbles them to Last.fm one part per day to stay under the daily limit. No extra packages needed. Vibe coded with Claude.
